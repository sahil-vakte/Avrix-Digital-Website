problem: Businesses were losing bookings to missed calls, especially outside office hours. Staff spent large parts of the day answering repeat questions and chasing follow-ups, and each location ran its own phone process.
objective: Answer every call, book appointments automatically, and give each business its own secure workspace on a shared platform.
solution: A multi-business voice AI platform. Each business gets its own workspace; an AI receptionist answers calls, books and reschedules, and hands complex calls to a person with full context. A campaigns module runs reminders and follow-ups, and a commercial module tracks usage through to billing.
features:
  - 24/7 AI call answering
  - Booking and rescheduling
  - Human handoff with context
  - Outbound reminder and follow-up campaigns
  - Consent capture on every call
  - Usage reconciliation and settlement
  - Durable background workers for reliability
phases:
  - title: Planning
    text: Call flows mapped for each business type, including FAQs, booking rules and escalation points.
  - title: Architecture
    text: Tenant isolation, the consent model and background worker design agreed before build.
  - title: Development
    text: Voice flows, business workspaces and the campaigns module built and tested on realistic calls.
  - title: Delivery
    text: Pilot with one business, tuning from real calls, then roll-out to further businesses.
secure: Each business has separate data, every call has a consent record, and a full audit trail shows what the AI did and when.
outcome: No call goes unanswered, bookings are captured out of hours, and staff time moves from the phone to patients and customers. A single platform now serves multiple businesses without mixing their data.
