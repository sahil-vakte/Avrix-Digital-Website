## The challenge

Most small and medium-sized businesses have antivirus and a firewall, and assume that's enough. But modern attacks often use stolen passwords and legitimate tools, so nothing flags them. Ransomware groups commonly strike at night or over a weekend, when nobody is looking. By Monday morning, files are encrypted and backups may be gone.

## Our approach

We put trained eyes on your systems 24/7. Alerts from your devices, network, cloud and email come into one place, where analysts separate real threats from noise. When something is genuinely wrong, we act quickly to contain it and tell you exactly what happened. Delivered by AVRIX with our specialist security team.
