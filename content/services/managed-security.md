## Why 24/7 monitoring matters

Most businesses have antivirus and a firewall and assume that's enough. But modern attacks often use stolen passwords and legitimate tools, so nothing obviously flags them. Ransomware groups commonly strike at night or over a weekend, when nobody is looking.

## How our SOC works

Alerts from your devices, network, cloud and email come into one place, where analysts separate real threats from noise. When something is genuinely wrong, we act quickly to contain it and tell you exactly what happened. Delivered by AVRIX with our specialist security team.
