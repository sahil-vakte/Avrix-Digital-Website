## Who it's for

Organisations without an in-house security team who need someone watching outside office hours, and those whose insurers, customers or regulators now expect continuous monitoring.

## Why it matters to your business

Modern attacks often use stolen passwords and legitimate tools, so antivirus doesn't flag them. Without someone watching, the first sign is often encrypted files on a Monday morning. Early detection is the difference between a short disruption and weeks of recovery.

## How AVRIX Digital does it

We start with the tools you already pay for, tune detection to your business so alerts mean something, and agree in advance exactly what we can do on your behalf. Delivered by AVRIX with our specialist security team.
