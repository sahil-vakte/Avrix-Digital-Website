## Why scope is everything

PCI DSS applies to every system that stores, processes or transmits card data, and everything connected to those systems. The more of your business that touches card data, the more expensive compliance becomes. Good design can shrink that scope dramatically.

## Our approach

We map how card data flows through your business, look for ways to take systems out of scope and then help you meet the requirements that remain. For e-commerce businesses, that often means a hosted or embedded payment option and the right controls on your payment pages.
