## Where AWS goes wrong

AWS is extraordinarily powerful, but it's easy to overspend on and hard to secure without expertise. The problems we see most: oversized servers running 24/7 for workloads that need them eight hours a day; no tagging, so nobody knows which project costs what; storage buckets with risky permissions; no tested backup or disaster recovery plan; and no cost alerts, so the bill is a surprise every month.

## Our approach

Our AWS Well-Architected Review examines your environment across operational excellence, security, reliability, performance and cost. You get a prioritised action plan that tackles the biggest wins first: lower spend, tighter security and better reliability. Managed service clients also get monthly cost reports, proactive monitoring and a named consultant.
