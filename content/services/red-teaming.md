## What is red teaming?

Attackers don't respect scope. They combine a phishing email, a weak password and a misconfigured server to reach their goal. Red teaming recreates that reality, so you can find out whether your defences work together, not just individually.

## How we run an exercise

Every exercise is planned with you, controlled throughout and fully authorised. Afterwards we replay the attack step by step with your team, showing exactly where it could have been stopped.
