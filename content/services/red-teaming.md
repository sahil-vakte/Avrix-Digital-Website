## Who it's for

Organisations with monitoring in place and regular testing behind them, that now want to know whether everything works together under pressure. Common in finance, healthcare, technology and larger professional services firms.

## Why it matters to your business

Real attackers don't stick to one system. They combine a convincing email, a reused password and a forgotten server. A red team exercise is the closest you can get to that experience without the damage.

## How AVRIX Digital does it

Every exercise is planned with you, controlled throughout and fully authorised. Afterwards we sit down with your team and walk through each step, showing exactly where the attack could have been stopped.
