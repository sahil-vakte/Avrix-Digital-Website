## Why test detection, not just defences

No organisation can block every attack. What separates a minor incident from a major breach is how quickly you notice and respond. A red team exercise tests that end to end: your technology, your monitoring and the people who have to act on an alert.

## Our approach

We design a realistic scenario around the threats your sector actually faces, run it in a controlled way with clear rules of engagement, and then sit down with your team to replay what happened. The goal is not to “win”. It is to leave your defenders better prepared.
