## Start with the business problem

You don't need a finished technical specification to start a conversation. Tell us what your team is trying to achieve, where work slows down and which tools you already use. Together we'll work out whether a custom application, an integration or a simpler improvement is the right next step.

## What could your application help you do?

- Replace repetitive spreadsheet processes with a shared workflow.
- Give customers or partners a dedicated portal to access information and submit requests.
- Help teams manage enquiries, tasks, approvals or operational records.
- Bring information from existing systems into one clearer view.
- Reduce duplicate data entry through suitable integrations.

## A scope that fits your priorities

For an SME, the first release should solve the most valuable problem. Before agreeing a project we cover users and permissions, data handling, integrations, hosting, testing, handover and ongoing support, so there are no surprises later.
