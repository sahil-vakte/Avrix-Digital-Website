## Who it's for

Organisations recovering from an attack, HR and legal teams dealing with suspected insider misuse, and insurers or law firms needing an independent technical account.

## Why it matters to your business

Without evidence you can only guess what was taken, how an attacker got in or who was responsible. Facts matter for regulator reports, insurance claims and any legal action.

## How AVRIX Digital does it

We preserve evidence first, then analyse it methodically. Reports are written for their audience: technical detail for IT, clear conclusions for leadership and lawyers.
