## Why forensics matters

After an incident, you will be asked hard questions by the ICO, your insurer, your customers and your board. How did they get in? What did they see? Are they still there? Guesswork is risky. Forensics gives you evidence-based answers.

## Our approach

We preserve evidence first, then analyse devices, cloud logs and mailboxes to build a clear timeline. Findings are explained in plain English, with the technical detail available for those who need it.
