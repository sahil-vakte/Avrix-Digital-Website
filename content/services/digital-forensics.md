## Why forensics matters

Without evidence, you can only guess what was taken, how the attacker got in, or who was responsible. Forensic investigation replaces guesswork with facts, which matters for regulator reports, insurance claims and legal action.

## How we investigate

We preserve evidence first, then analyse it methodically, building a timeline of what happened. Reports are written for the audience: technical detail for IT, clear conclusions for leadership and lawyers.
