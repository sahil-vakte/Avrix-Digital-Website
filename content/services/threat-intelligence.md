## Why threat intelligence matters

Attackers do their research. They collect leaked passwords, map your systems and study your staff on social media. Threat intelligence lets you see the same picture first, so you can close the gaps before they're used.

## How it works

We combine automated monitoring with analyst review, so you only receive findings that are real and relevant, each with a clear recommended action.
