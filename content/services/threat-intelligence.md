## Why intelligence matters

Many attacks are prepared in the open: passwords from old breaches are tested against your systems, look-alike domains are registered for phishing and new software flaws are exploited within days. Knowing about these early gives you time to act.

## Our approach

We monitor the sources that matter for your business, filter out the noise and send short, practical alerts and monthly briefings. Each one explains what we found, why it matters and what to do next.
