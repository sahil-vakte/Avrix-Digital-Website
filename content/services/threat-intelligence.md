## Who it's for

Organisations with a recognisable brand, customers who could be targeted by impersonation scams, or ISO 27001 and NIS2 obligations to track the threats they face.

## Why it matters to your business

Attackers do their homework. They collect leaked passwords, register lookalike domains and study your staff online. Seeing the same picture first lets you close the gaps before they're used.

## How AVRIX Digital does it

Automated monitoring finds the signals; an analyst checks each one before it reaches you. You only hear about findings that are real and relevant, each with a clear next step.
