## Who it's for

Any organisation where staff use email, especially finance teams who handle payments, and businesses preparing for ISO 27001, NIS2 or a cyber insurance renewal.

## Why it matters to your business

Email filters stop a lot, but not everything. One click on a fake Microsoft login page can hand over a mailbox, and with it invoices, customer details and access to other systems. A single fraudulent bank transfer can wipe out a month's profit.

## How AVRIX Digital does it

We keep practice regular, realistic and short. Scenarios reflect the scams your sector actually sees, lessons take minutes, and reporting shows where to focus support.
