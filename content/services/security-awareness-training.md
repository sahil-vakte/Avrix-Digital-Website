## The challenge

Spam filters stop a lot, but not everything. Attackers now write convincing, personalised emails, and staff are busy. One click on a fake Microsoft login page can hand over a mailbox, and with it access to invoices, customer data and other systems. Annual tick-box training rarely changes behaviour.

## Our approach

We make security awareness regular, realistic and short. Simulations mirror the scams your industry actually sees. Training is brief and practical, and reporting shows where risk sits so you can help the people who need it. Over time, your team becomes much harder to fool.
