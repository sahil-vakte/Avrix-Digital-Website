## Why awareness matters

Spam filters stop a lot, but not everything. Attackers write convincing, personalised emails, and staff are busy. One click on a fake Microsoft login page can hand over a mailbox, and with it access to invoices, customer data and other systems.

## How the programme works

We make security awareness regular, realistic and short. Simulations mirror the scams your sector sees. Training is brief and practical, and reporting shows where risk sits so you can help the people who need it.
