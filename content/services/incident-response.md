## The challenge

A cyber attack is chaotic. Files won't open, emails are going out that nobody sent, or a supplier calls to say their payment never arrived. Decisions made in the first few hours, such as wiping a laptop or restoring from an infected backup, can make recovery much harder and destroy the evidence needed to understand what happened.

## Our approach

We bring calm and order. First we contain the attack. Then we investigate carefully, preserving evidence, and restore your most important systems first. Throughout, we explain what's happening in plain English and help you meet your reporting duties. Afterwards, we close the gaps that let the attacker in.
