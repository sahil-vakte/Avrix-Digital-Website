## Why preparation matters

The businesses that recover best from cyber incidents have three things in common: backups that work, a plan people have practised and someone to call. Without them, decisions are made in a panic, evidence is lost and recovery takes much longer.

## Our approach

When an incident happens, we focus on containment first, then investigation and safe recovery, keeping you informed in plain English throughout. Before anything happens, we help you build the plan, test your backups and run practice exercises.
