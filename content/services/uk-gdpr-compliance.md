## Why data protection is a security issue

Most data protection problems come down to security basics: too many people with access, data kept longer than needed, unencrypted laptops or tracking switched on without consent. Policies matter, but they only work if the technology behind them is set up properly.

## Our approach

We combine data protection know-how with the hands-on technical skills of our marketing, cloud and security teams. That means we can fix your cookie banner, tighten Microsoft 365 permissions and write the privacy notice, rather than handing you a list of recommendations.
