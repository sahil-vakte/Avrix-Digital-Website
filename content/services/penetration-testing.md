## Why scanners alone are not enough

Automated scanners are good at spotting missing patches and known misconfigurations. They are poor at understanding how your application is meant to work. They will not notice that changing a number in a web address shows another customer's invoice, or that a discount code can be applied twice. Those business-logic flaws are exactly what attackers look for, and they need a person to find them.

## Our approach

We combine automated scanning with hands-on manual testing, following OWASP methodology for applications and NCSC guidance for infrastructure. You get a report you can actually use: a short summary for decision-makers, detailed technical findings for your developers and a retest to confirm the fixes worked.
