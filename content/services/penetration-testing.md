## The challenge

Most breaches at small and medium-sized businesses don't use anything clever. They use a forgotten test page, a weak password on a remote access tool, a cloud storage bucket left open or an app that shows one customer's data to another. Nobody noticed because nobody looked. By the time it's found, the cost is lost data, lost trust and, often, a report to the ICO.

## Our approach

We look at your systems the way an attacker would, then explain what we found the way a business owner needs to hear it. Every finding comes with the risk in plain terms, the evidence, and the steps to fix it. You get one report for the board and one for the people doing the fixing, and we stay on hand until the issues are closed.
