## Why endpoints and identities

Most attacks start with a person: a phishing email, a reused password, a malicious attachment. That means the first signs show up on a laptop or in a sign-in log. Catching and containing threats there is the most effective way to stop an incident growing.

## Our approach

We deploy and manage endpoint detection and response, connect identity signals from Entra ID or Google Workspace and investigate alerts as they arrive. When we confirm a threat, we contain it using the actions you have approved and guide you through recovery.
