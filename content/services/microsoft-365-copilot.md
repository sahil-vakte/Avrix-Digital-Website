## Why Microsoft 365 often underdelivers

The most common problem isn't that businesses don't have Microsoft 365. It's that it's set up wrong. Licences don't match real needs: some businesses pay for Premium features they never use, while others sit on Basic plans that leave them exposed. Intune isn't configured, so company data lives on unmanaged personal devices. Security features are left switched off. And Copilot gets installed but never used because nobody showed the team how.

Our Microsoft 365 assessment reviews your whole tenant, finds the gaps and gives you a clear plan. For new setups and migrations we handle everything: licence supply, data migration, Intune enrolment, security baseline and Copilot training. You get one partner from start to finish.
