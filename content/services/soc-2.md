## Who it's for

UK and European SaaS and technology businesses expanding into the US, or selling to multinationals that use SOC 2 as their standard.

## Why it matters to your business

Without a SOC 2 report, US deals often stall at the security review. Preparing well means the audit becomes a formality rather than a months-long scramble.

## How AVRIX Digital does it

We scope SOC 2 around what your customers actually need, design controls with your engineers rather than for them, and keep evidence organised so the Type II period runs smoothly.
