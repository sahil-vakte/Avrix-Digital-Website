## What is SOC 2?

SOC 2 is an assurance report on how a service organisation protects customer data, based on criteria covering security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality and privacy. It's the standard ask from US enterprise buyers.

## How we help

We scope SOC 2 around what your customers actually need, build controls that don't slow your engineers down, and keep evidence organised so the Type II period runs smoothly.
