## Who it's for

Organisations with offices, servers, remote workers or a mix of on-premises and cloud systems, particularly those preparing for Cyber Essentials Plus or ISO 27001, or renewing cyber insurance.

## Why it matters to your business

An exposed remote desktop service or an unpatched VPN can turn into days of downtime. Once inside, attackers look for weak Active Directory settings that let them take control of everything. Knowing where those paths are lets you close them cheaply, before they're used.

## How AVRIX Digital does it

We follow recognised testing methods mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, adapt them to your environment, and explain each finding in terms of what it would mean for your operations.
