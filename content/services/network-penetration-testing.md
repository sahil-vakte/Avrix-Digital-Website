## Why network testing matters

Ransomware groups rarely break in through something clever. They use an exposed remote desktop service, an unpatched VPN or a reused password, then move quietly across the network until they reach the systems that matter. Network testing finds those weaknesses before they're used against you.

## How we test

We follow recognised methods, mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK framework, and adapt them to your environment. Testing is controlled and agreed in advance, and every finding comes with clear evidence and practical steps to fix it.
