## Why SOC 2 comes up

US enterprise customers use SOC 2 reports to decide whether a supplier can be trusted with their data. Without one, deals slow down while security teams send long questionnaires. With one, many of those questions are answered before they are asked.

## Our approach

We scope your SOC 2 sensibly, design controls that fit a growing technology business and set up tools that collect evidence automatically. Where your platform runs on AWS or Google Cloud, our cloud team can help put the technical controls in place.
