## Why ISO 27001 projects stall

Many ISO 27001 projects get stuck writing documents. Policies are copied from templates, nobody follows them and the first audit becomes a scramble. The standard is really about running a consistent process for understanding and managing risk.

## Our approach

We start with your risks and your existing ways of working, then build the ISMS around them. Because our team also works hands-on with Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and AWS, we can put the technical controls in place, not just describe them.
