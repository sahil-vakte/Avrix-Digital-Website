## Who it's for

EU organisations in NIS2 sectors, EU financial entities under DORA, UK operators of essential services, and UK technology and managed service providers whose EU customers are passing these requirements down the chain.

## Why it matters to your business

These rules make senior management personally accountable for cyber risk and set tight incident reporting deadlines. Even organisations outside their direct scope feel the effect through supplier contracts and due diligence.

## How AVRIX Digital does it

We confirm exactly what applies before recommending anything, then build on frameworks you may already have, such as ISO 27001 or Cyber Essentials, to avoid duplicated effort.
