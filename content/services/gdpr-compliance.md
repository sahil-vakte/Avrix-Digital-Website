## Who it's for

Any organisation handling customer, patient, employee or supporter data in the UK or EU, particularly those facing customer audits, DPA negotiations or a recent near-miss.

## Why it matters to your business

Fines make headlines, but the bigger cost is usually lost trust, stalled deals and the disruption of a poorly handled breach. Most data protection failures are really security failures.

We combine data protection process with hands-on security expertise, and write everything in plain English for the people who need to follow it.
