## Why hiring tech and digital talent is so hard

Demand for cloud, DevOps, data, security and digital marketing skills keeps outstripping supply. The best candidates juggle several offers and usually accept the one that moves fastest, not the one that pays most. Generic agencies make it worse by sending CVs of whoever is available rather than whoever is right, so hiring managers waste hours on interviews that go nowhere.

## Our approach

We take the time to understand your business, culture and team before approaching a single candidate. We brief candidates properly, so when they apply they're already bought in. We only present people we'd personally vouch for. And we stay with you through the offer, the counter-offer and the first 90 days, because a successful placement is someone still thriving a year later.
