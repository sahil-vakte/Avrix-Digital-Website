## Why many SIEMs disappoint

The common story is a SIEM bought for compliance, connected to a few log sources and left to generate alerts nobody has time to read. The result is cost without protection. The value comes from choosing the right logs, writing detections that fit your business and having someone act on them.

## Our approach

We start with what you need to detect and what auditors will ask for, design the logging around that and tune the detections until they are meaningful. Then we either monitor it for you or train your team to run it.
