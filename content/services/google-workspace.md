## The common pitfalls

Plenty of businesses have a Google Workspace that technically works but isn't configured properly. Email authentication records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) are missing, so emails land in spam. Shared Drives aren't used, so files live in personal drives and leave with staff. Security sits on default settings. And billing is higher than it should be because licences don't match how people work.

## Our approach

Our setup and audit service fixes all of this systematically. We configure your domain, structure your Shared Drives by team, apply security best practice, right-size your licences and brief your team on how to use it well. For migrations we move everything (mailboxes, files, calendars and contacts) with no data loss and minimal downtime.
