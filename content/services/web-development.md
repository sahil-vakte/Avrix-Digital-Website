## Why most business websites underdeliver

Most SME websites share three problems. They're slow, so they fail Google's Core Web Vitals. They're not truly mobile-friendly, even though most visitors arrive on a phone. And they aren't built to turn visitors into enquiries. A beautiful site that doesn't rank or generate leads is an expensive brochure, not a business tool.

## Our approach

We start from the outcome you need: more leads, more sales, more trust. We map your customer journey before writing a line of code, build technical SEO into the architecture, configure GA4 for every key action, and test on real mobile devices before launch. The result looks good and performs.
