## What is Cyber Essentials?

Cyber Essentials covers five technical controls: firewalls, secure configuration, user access control, malware protection and security update management. Together they defend against the most common internet-based attacks. Central government contracts that handle personal information usually require it.

## How we help

We start with a quick review so you know exactly where you stand, then fix the gaps with your team rather than handing over a list. When you're ready, we prepare the evidence, coordinate certification and keep you compliant through the year.
