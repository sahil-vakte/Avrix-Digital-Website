## Who it's for

UK organisations bidding for public sector or larger corporate contracts, suppliers whose customers have asked for it, and anyone who wants a recognised security baseline at modest cost.

## Why it matters to your business

Cyber Essentials covers five areas: firewalls, secure configuration, user access, malware protection and keeping software updated. Central government contracts that handle personal information usually require it. Without it, some bids simply aren't possible.

## How AVRIX Digital does it

We start with an honest check, then roll up our sleeves. Because we also set up Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace security, we can often make the changes ourselves rather than hand you a to-do list.
