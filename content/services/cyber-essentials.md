## Why Cyber Essentials matters

The scheme focuses on five technical controls that protect against the most common attacks: firewalls, secure configuration, user access control, malware protection and keeping software up to date. It is required for many central government contracts, and more customers and insurers now ask for it as standard.

## Our approach

We check your devices, accounts and cloud services against the requirements, fix what needs fixing (often in Microsoft 365, Google Workspace or Intune, which our team supports every day) and guide you through the questionnaire and assessment. You get the certificate, and a setup that genuinely stays secure.
