## The challenge

Cyber Essentials sounds simple, and the five controls are. The trouble is applying them to a real business: laptops nobody has updated, shared logins, old routers, staff using personal phones for work email. The question set is easy to answer wrongly, and a failed submission costs time when a tender deadline is close.

## Our approach

We start with a quick review so you know exactly where you stand, then fix the gaps with your team rather than handing over a list. Because we also set up Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, we can make many of the changes directly. When you're ready, we prepare the evidence and coordinate certification, then keep you compliant through the year.
