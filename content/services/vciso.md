## Who it's for

Founders, managing directors and boards of growing organisations who know cyber risk matters but don't have a senior person to own it.

## Why it matters to your business

When security falls to whoever runs IT, strategy gets lost in day-to-day tickets. The board gets little useful information, questionnaires take days and money goes on tools that don't address the biggest risks.

## How AVRIX Digital does it

Your vCISO learns your business, agrees priorities with leadership and keeps the plan moving month after month. Reports focus on business risk, so directors can make informed decisions.
