## The challenge

Cyber security often falls to whoever manages IT, on top of everything else. Strategy gets lost in day-to-day tickets. The board gets little useful information, customer questionnaires take days to answer, and money goes on tools that don't address the biggest risks.

## Our approach

We give security a senior owner. Your virtual CISO learns your business, agrees priorities with leadership and keeps the plan moving month after month. Reports focus on business risk, not technical detail, so directors can make informed decisions. Delivered by AVRIX with our specialist security team.
