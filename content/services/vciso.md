## Why a virtual CISO?

Cyber security often falls to whoever manages IT, on top of everything else. Strategy gets lost in day-to-day tickets, the board gets little useful information and money goes on tools that don't address the biggest risks.

## How it works

Your vCISO learns your business, agrees priorities with leadership and keeps the plan moving month after month. Reports focus on business risk, not technical detail, so directors can make informed decisions.
