## Why monitoring matters

Most attacks leave traces long before the damage is done: a login from an unusual country, a new admin account, a laptop talking to a known bad server. Without someone watching, those signs are missed until files are encrypted or data appears online.

## Our approach

We connect your most important systems, tune the alerts to your business and monitor them continuously. When something looks wrong, a person investigates, and you get a clear explanation and recommended action, not a raw alert. Monthly reports show what we saw, what we did and what to improve.
