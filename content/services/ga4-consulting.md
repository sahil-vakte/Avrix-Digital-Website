## The challenge

When Universal Analytics was switched off, most businesses rushed GA4 in without proper configuration. The result records sessions and page views but misses the events that matter: enquiries, calls, purchases, bookings. Without them your marketing can't prove what's working, ad platforms can't optimise properly, and decisions get made on gut feel.

## Our approach

Our audit pinpoints every gap in your tracking. We then rebuild your configuration properly: key events, conversions, consent mode and connections to your ad platforms and CRM. You end up with a clean, complete data set and a Looker Studio dashboard that shows business performance in plain English, in real time.
