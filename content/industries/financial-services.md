## Why financial services are targeted

Financial firms hold exactly what criminals want: money, identities and access to client accounts. Many smaller firms rely on email and a handful of cloud platforms, which makes mailbox takeover and invoice fraud a constant threat.

## How we help

We combine cyber security, compliance support, cloud administration and compliant marketing in one team, so your security, systems and growth plans work together rather than against each other.
