## Why healthcare needs extra care

Healthcare providers combine sensitive data, critical availability and limited IT resource. A single compromised mailbox or ransomware attack can stop bookings, delay treatment and lead to ICO investigations.

## How we help

We secure the systems your team uses every day, support your compliance obligations and help you grow with marketing that respects healthcare advertising rules, all through one point of contact.
