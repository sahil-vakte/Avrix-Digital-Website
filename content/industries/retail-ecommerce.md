## Why e-commerce needs growth and security together

Every marketing pound you spend sends people to your checkout. If tracking is wrong, you cannot tell which campaigns work. If the checkout is compromised, customers lose money and you lose trust. Growth and security are part of the same job.

## How we help

Our marketing, analytics, web and security teams work together, so your campaigns, your tracking and your store protection support each other.
