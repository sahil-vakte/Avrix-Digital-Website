## Why education is a target

Education providers have lots of users, lots of devices and valuable personal data, but rarely the security budget of a large business. Phishing, account takeover and ransomware are all common.

## How we help

We focus on the controls that make the biggest difference, set up your cloud platforms safely and help you recruit learners online, all planned around the academic calendar.
