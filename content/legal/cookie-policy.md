## What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Some are essential for a website to work. Others help us understand how the site is used, and we only set those with your consent.

## Cookies we use

| Category | Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strictly necessary | `avrix_consent` | AVRIX Digital | Remembers your cookie choices | 6 months |
| Strictly necessary | Turnstile cookies | Cloudflare | Protects forms from spam bots | Session |
| Analytics (consent) | `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics 4 | Measures how visitors use the site | Up to 13 months |
| Advertising (consent) | `_gcl_au` and platform cookies | Google Ads / Meta / LinkedIn [only if used] | Measures ad performance | Up to 13 months |

We also store first-visit campaign information (such as UTM tags) in your browser's session storage, so that if you send an enquiry we know which campaign brought you. It's cleared when you close the tab.

## Changing your choices

Use the **Cookie preferences** link in the website footer at any time. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
