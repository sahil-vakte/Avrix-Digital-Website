Owner-supplied brand assets:
- `avrix-mark.webp`: the AVRIX "A" mark (cyan, blue and purple gradient) with transparency, used in the header and footer next to the wordmark.
- `avrix-logo-navy.webp`: mark plus navy AVRIX wordmark, for light backgrounds and search metadata.
- `avrix-logo-white.webp`: mark plus white AVRIX wordmark, for dark backgrounds and social preview images.
- `avrix-favicon.webp`: the mark on a square transparent canvas, used for favicons and app icons.

All four were produced from the owner's supplied AVRIX.png logo (November 2026). `content/site.json` selects these assets. The preparation script creates the favicon set, Apple touch icon, web manifest and search logo.
